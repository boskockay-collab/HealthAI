## Project Title

HealthAI – Intelligent Health Assistant

**Author:** Trevor Felix

**Year:** 2026

---

## Introduction

HealthAI is an AI-powered health assistant designed to help users
understand common symptoms and receive general health information.

The user enters symptoms in natural language, and the system analyzes
the information to identify possible symptom patterns.

The system also checks for potentially serious warning signs and
provides appropriate safety guidance.

---

## Problem Statement

Many people experience symptoms but may not immediately understand
what they could mean or when they should seek medical attention.

HealthAI provides a simple way for users to enter their symptoms
and receive general information about possible symptom patterns.

---

## Project Objectives

1. To allow users to enter symptoms using natural language.

2. To process and analyze symptoms entered by the user.

3. To identify possible symptom patterns.

4. To detect potentially serious warning signs.

5. To provide general health guidance.

6. To encourage users to seek professional medical care when necessary.

---

## How the System Works

User enters symptoms

↓

HealthAI processes the text

↓

Emergency detector checks for warning signs

↓

Symptom analyzer compares symptoms with the health knowledge base

↓

Possible symptom patterns are identified

↓

General health guidance is displayed

↓

The user is advised when professional medical care may be necessary

---

## Artificial Intelligence Used

HealthAI uses simple Artificial Intelligence and Natural Language
Processing concepts.

The system processes human language entered by the user and compares
the words and phrases with predefined symptom patterns.

It uses rule-based matching to identify relevant symptoms.

---

## Emergency Detection

HealthAI checks for potentially serious warning signs such as:

- Difficulty breathing
- Severe chest pain
- Loss of consciousness
- Seizures
- Sudden weakness
- Severe bleeding
- Severe allergic reactions

When a possible emergency sign is detected, the system displays a
warning advising the user to seek urgent medical attention.

---

## Health Knowledge Base

The system contains general information about common symptom patterns,
including:

- Common cold
- Flu-like illness
- Malaria-like symptoms
- Tension headache
- Migraine-like symptoms
- Sore throat
- Gastrointestinal infection
- Dehydration
- Allergic symptoms
- Urinary tract infection-like symptoms

---

## Limitations

1. HealthAI does not provide a medical diagnosis.

2. It uses a limited knowledge base.

3. Its analysis is based on predefined symptom patterns.

4. It may not recognize every possible description of a symptom.

5. It cannot replace a qualified healthcare professional.

## Safety Disclaimer

HealthAI is an educational health information system.

It does not diagnose diseases, prescribe treatment, or replace a
qualified healthcare professional.

Users experiencing severe or rapidly worsening symptoms should seek
appropriate medical attention.
